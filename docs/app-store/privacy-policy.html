WKCC Perks Privacy Policy

Wilmette/Kenilworth Chamber of Commerce (“Chamber”, “we”, “us”)
Last updated: July 29, 2026

WKCC Perks (“the App”) is a private membership app for active Wilmette/Kenilworth Chamber of Commerce business members. This policy describes what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Membership email used to sign in (must match an eligible chamber membership record).
  • Authentication data such as one-time passcodes, session tokens, and last-login metadata.
  • Profile and business listing fields you choose to provide in the App (for example logo, category, short description, website, phone, and address visibility preferences).
  • Promotion submissions and related content you submit for chamber review.
  • Device and diagnostic data that Apple or our hosting providers may process as part of normal app operation (for example crash diagnostics if you opt in via the system).

How we use information

  • To verify chamber membership and sign you in to the App.
  • To display member perks, the business directory, and your member profile.
  • To review and publish member-submitted promotions.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to operate the App (for example cloud hosting and transactional email for sign-in codes). Information may be visible to other authenticated members when you publish a business listing or approved promotion. Chamber staff with admin access may review submissions and manage perks.

Data retention

We retain account, listing, and promotion data while your membership is active and as needed to operate the App. Sign-in codes are short-lived. You may contact the Chamber to request correction or removal of app-managed profile fields where applicable.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for chamber business members and is not directed to children under 13.
